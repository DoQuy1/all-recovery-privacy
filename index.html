Files on your device

To find recoverable photos, videos, audio and documents, the app reads your device storage, including trash folders, caches, thumbnails and hidden folders.

When you recover a file, a new copy is saved on your device (for example Pictures/AllRecovery). Your files, file names and file contents are never uploaded, sold or shared.

💬

Deleted messages (optional)

This feature works only after you turn on Notification Access. The app then saves the text of notifications from the chat apps you choose in its private storage on your device, so you can read messages that the sender later deleted.

Saved messages never leave your device. Delete a conversation in the app, turn off Notification Access, or uninstall the app to remove them.

🔑

Permissions we use

  • All files access / storage: scan for and recover deleted files.
  • Notification access (optional): save messages from the chat apps you choose.
  • Notifications (optional): show the quick access notification.
  • USB access (optional): read an SD card or USB drive you connect, read-only.
  • Kill background processes: used only by the RAM booster. It never reads data from other apps.
  • Internet: used only to load ads.

🚫

Data we don't collect

No account is required. We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, videos, documents or messages on any server.

🧒

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

🛡️

Security & retention

Recovered files and saved messages stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Temporary data from a scan is kept in the app's cache and removed when a new scan starts.
